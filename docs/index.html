Fastener racks

Three 6 × 2 Gridfinity racks on 1 inch pegboard. M2 to M5 stainless, two-colour Fillaprint labels.

Racks

M3

12 bins, 18 compartments

M2 · M2.5 · M4

12 bins, 20 compartments

M5

8 bins, 9 compartments

Labels

  • Front face: thread size, then symbol, dimension line and length. Inside back wall: the same, small.
  • Screws are side profiles; the dimension line runs where the length is measured.
  • Fillaprint at the 0.42 mm reference size: every stroke and gap is at least two lines wide.
  • Labels are flush inlays 0.84 mm deep, on walls thickened by the same amount.

Print

  • 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.42 mm line width, 0.20 mm layers. Bins: filament 1 body, filament 2 labels. Racks: one filament.
  • Pegboard: 1 inch grid, ¼ inch holes, ¼ inch board. Two upturned hooks and two pins per rack; racks 11 inches apart.
  • Rack shells sit on Gridfinity pockets; bins lift out.
